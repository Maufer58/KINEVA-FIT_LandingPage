User guide — KINEVA FIT

Version: 1.2 · Date: 4 October 2026
Audience: app users (all plans, including Free)
Published languages: Italian · English (for other UI languages)

This guide covers how to use the app only. It does not include development, Mac install, or technical setup steps.

Where to find it: Settings → Base → Guides & help · or on the KINEVA FIT website (footer → Guides).

1. What KINEVA FIT is

KINEVA FIT helps you create and follow workout routines, keep history, and (on Pro plans) manage multiple clients.

It is not a medical device. “Attention areas” are training preferences, not diagnoses.

Platforms: Android · iPhone · iPad · Apple Watch (Watch features from Athlete Pro).

2. First launch

  1. Open the Privacy Policy and EULA and confirm you have read them.
  2. Accept the training disclaimer.
  3. Complete onboarding (language and basic tips).
  4. (Optional) Start the free trial or pick a plan.

Onboarding also shows where to open this guide later.

5. Home and routines

  • New routine: Home → New Routine (editor, AI, photo, template, free workout; options depend on plan).
  • Groups: organize several routines together.
  • ▶: starts the workout.
  • ⋯: duplicate, archive, template (not Free), schedule, export, send to Watch (Athlete Pro+).

Manage mode

Home → Manage → select routines or groups → Send to Watch / Select all / Delete → ✕ to exit.

Free limits

1 active routine · 1 group · max 15 exercises · no distance/GPS.

6. Routine editor

  1. Add exercises from the library.
  2. In the library you can filter by type: All · Fitness · Mobility · Pilates.
    - All shows only what you have already unlocked.
    - Mobility and Pilates are always browseable: if the pack/group is locked you see a clear preview label (Pilates pack or Mobility group) and cannot add it to the routine yet.
  3. Set sets, reps, load, rests (and advanced methods if your plan includes them).
  4. Distance / GPS measures require Athlete Pro+.
  5. Save: the routine appears on Home.

6bis. Exercise library and detail card

The catalog includes hundreds of exercises (fitness, mobility, cardio/swim and, with the add-on, Pilates). Each entry has a Gym Visual GIF, description, execution tips and a muscle map.

From the library or a routine, open the exercise detail to see:

Item What it shows
GIF Movement animation (Gym Visual)
Muscle groups — Primary Main muscles of the movement
Muscle groups — Secondary Synergists and stabilizers (when listed)
Silhouette Highlighted zones aligned with primary/secondary tags (front/back)
Description / execution Movement steps and practical tips
Equipment / unsuitable for Setup and cautions tied to profile attention areas

Muscle labels and silhouettes are teaching simplifications: they do not replace professional assessment. On Home and in the editor you can also toggle the routine muscle map (macro bar or silhouette) for a quick view of what the routine targets.

7. Runner (workout)

  1. From Home tap ▶.
  2. Follow timers, rests and voice cues.
  3. Routines with distance/GPS: Athlete Pro+ only.
  4. After the session, see the summary in History.

8. Apple Watch (Athlete Pro+)

Requirements: Athlete Pro plan (or active trial), KINEVA FIT on iPhone and Watch, Bluetooth on, Watch paired.

  1. From iPhone: Watch app → KINEVA FIT → Install.
  2. Open KINEVA FIT on the Watch at least once.
  3. Send a routine: Home → ⋯ → Send to Apple Watch (or Manage mode for several).
  4. On the Watch: open the routine → OPEN → START.

If you see “not installed” but the icon is there: open the app on the Watch and send again.

Add the watch face complication via Edit face → KINEVA FIT.

13. Trainer and Studio (Piano Pro / Studio & Gym)

For personal trainers and gyms:

  • Clients from the drawer (New client): in the profile you can add, edit and remove physical notes / limitations at any time (they stay on the client as operator reminders).
  • 4-digit PIN to protect the area (keep the recovery code).
  • Assign routines, PDF/JSON export, exercise database (muscle / equipment / type from fixed catalog lists only).
  • Every catalog entry includes GIF, primary/secondary muscles, silhouette, description and execution tips (same content as the athlete library).
  • Studio: unlimited clients, mobility protocols, multi-trainer, full PDF encyclopedia (entire catalog; muscle segments remain on Pro Plan too).
  • Print / PDF export of the exercise library (segment manuals or full encyclopedia): paid Pro Plan / Studio subscription only — not during the free trial.
  • Deliveries & Backup, history/biometrics CSV, full backup, history ZIP archive, and personal JSON export: paid subscription only — not during the free trial.
  • Routine PDF/image exports use silhouettes and YouTube QR; Gym Visual GIFs stay in-app only (never printed or exported).
  • Single-routine export (table/manual PDF, HD image) and progress reports (JSON/PDF): paid subscription only — not during the free trial.
  • Colleague JSON import: paid Pro Plan / Studio only — not during trial.
  • AI credits in trial: max 10 (not the full plan 50/150); paid boosters remain available.

On iPad the trainer area is especially convenient for catalog and handoffs.
