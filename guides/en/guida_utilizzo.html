User guide — KINEVA FIT

Version: 1.9 · Date: 25 October 2026
Audience: app users (all plans, including Free)
Published languages: Italian · English (for other UI languages)

This guide covers how to use the app only. It does not include development, Mac install, or technical setup steps.

Where to find it: tap the logo in the header (section for the current screen) · Settings → Base → Guides & help · or on the KINEVA FIT website (footer → Guides).

1. What KINEVA FIT is

KINEVA FIT helps you create and follow workout routines, keep history, and (on Pro plans) manage multiple clients.

It is not a medical device. “Attention areas” are training preferences, not diagnoses.

Platforms: Android · iPhone · iPad · Apple Watch (Watch features from Athlete Pro).

2. First launch

  1. Open the Privacy Policy and EULA and confirm you have read them.
  2. Accept the training disclaimer.
  3. Complete onboarding (language and basic tips).
  4. (Optional) Start the free trial or pick a plan.

Onboarding also shows where to open this guide later: tap the logo in the header (or Settings → Base → Guides & help).

5. Home and routines

  • New routine: Home → New Routine (editor, AI, photo, template, free workout; options depend on plan).
  • Groups: organize several routines together.
  • ▶: starts the workout.
  • ⋯: duplicate, archive, template (not Free), schedule, export, send to Watch (Athlete Pro+).

Manage mode

Home → Manage → select routines or groups → Send to Watch / Select all / Delete → ✕ to exit.

Free limits

1 active routine · 1 group · max 15 exercises · no distance/GPS.

6. Routine editor

  1. Add exercises from the library.
  2. In the library you can filter by type: All · Fitness · Mobility · Pilates.
    - All shows only what you have already unlocked.
    - Mobility is included from Trainer Pro. Below that plan the entry stays locked and does not open: it goes to the plans. Mobility exercises in the library cannot be selected.
    - Pilates can be browsed as a preview. Using it in a routine needs Athlete Pro+ and the pack; purchase asks “Do you want to continue?” and starts only on Confirm.
  3. Set sets, reps, load, rests (and advanced methods if your plan includes them).
  4. Distance / GPS measures require Athlete Pro+.
  5. Save: the routine appears on Home.
    (End-of-workout reminder and gym-bag checklist are global settings: Settings → Base.)

6bis. Exercise library and detail card

The catalog includes hundreds of exercises (fitness, mobility, cardio/swim and, with the add-on, Pilates). Each entry has a Gym Visual GIF, description, execution tips and a muscle map.

In the library, exercise picker, routine editor, Home and Runner: tap the GIF icon (or the info button) to open the exercise detail with description and notes:

Item What it shows
GIF Movement animation (Gym Visual)
Muscle groups — Primary Main muscles of the movement
Muscle groups — Secondary Synergists and stabilizers (when listed)
Silhouette Highlighted zones aligned with primary/secondary tags (front/back)
Description / execution Movement steps and practical tips
Equipment / unsuitable for Setup and cautions tied to profile attention areas

Muscle labels and silhouettes are teaching simplifications: they do not replace professional assessment. On Home and in the editor you can also toggle the routine muscle map (macro bar or silhouette) for a quick view of what the routine targets.

7. Runner (workout)

  1. From Home tap ▶.
  2. (Optional, at home) If bag checklist is on in Settings → Base, Home shows a bag icon: open it before you leave, tick items and listen to the voice guide. It does not appear when you tap ▶ (you’re already at the gym).
  3. Follow timers, rests and voice cues.
  4. Pause / Resume: with auto-advance (or T21), double-tap the screen (same as Apple Watch), including during rest; the blue ▶ button remains only when auto-advance is off. Stops voice guidance and says Paused; on resume it says Resuming. An orange PAUSED banner appears under the top bar (Double-tap to resume).
  5. Exercise navigation: the labeled Previous exercise / Next exercise buttons (and ⏮ / ⏭ in the app bar) jump between exercises; the bottom ↺ control replays the set (during rest it cancels rest and redos the set just finished; during work it goes to the previous set or restarts the current one); ✓ completes the step. SKIP marks the current exercise as skipped.
  6. Routines with distance/GPS: Athlete Pro+ only.
  7. After the session, see the summary in History.
  8. End-of-workout reminder (optional, global): in Settings → Base you can turn it on/off. Factory default is ON with a message (water bottle, towel, locker key — editable). When on, it appears on the summary and is spoken (including Apple Watch).

8. Apple Watch (Athlete Pro+)

Requirements: Athlete Pro plan (or active trial), KINEVA FIT on iPhone and Watch, Bluetooth on, Watch paired.

  1. From iPhone: Watch app → KINEVA FIT → Install.
  2. Open KINEVA FIT on the Watch at least once.
  3. Send a routine: Home → ⋯ → Send to Apple Watch (or Manage mode for several).
  4. On the Watch: open the routine → OPEN → START.

During a workout, double-tap the screen to pause and resume (the Pause button in Options does the same): the clock stops and restarts, and voice guidance is cut. The top-left ✕ on the exercise screen, including while the end-of-session voice is speaking, cuts the guide immediately, ends the session, and stops HealthKit (or use End in Options). If Options / parameters or the exercise list is already open, ✕ only dismisses that sheet and returns to the live exercise.

Pool swimming (lap measure): on the Watch, HealthKit uses a pool swim session with the routine’s pool length (e.g. 25 m). Lap counts and pool length sync back to History on iPhone.

At the end of a workout the Watch asks for effort from 1 (light) to 5 (maximum). The number stays on that session in iPhone History, where you can also change it.

If you see “not installed” but the icon is there: open the app on the Watch and send again.

Add the watch face complication via Edit face → KINEVA FIT.

9. Calendar, data and history

  • Calendar: schedule and (Pro/Studio) appointments.
  • Data: training and biometric charts.
  • History: completed sessions, filters, detail; GPS map if present (Athlete Pro+). On each session you can set effort from 1 to 5 and write notes, free text or the chips Easy, As expected, Hard, Pain, Short on time. They stay on the phone.

History export/import (ZIP): Settings → Base → Export / Import · History, or Trainer Pro → Export & import → History (Athlete Pro+, not during trial).

13. Trainer and Studio (Piano Pro / Studio & Gym)

For personal trainers and gyms:

  • Clients from the drawer (New client): in the profile you can add, edit and remove physical notes / limitations at any time (they stay on the client as operator reminders).
  • 4-digit PIN to protect the area. The 8-digit recovery code appears only once: Copy to clipboard puts it on the clipboard (paste it into Notes); I've written it down closes the window and does not save it in the app. On the sign-in screen you can type it, use the keypad, or Paste code for the 8-digit recovery code. In Settings → PIN you can set automatic sign-out: if you do not touch the screen for 1, 3, 5, 15 or 30 minutes, the app asks for the PIN again. Never turns off only that timer; sending the app to the background still locks it. The Face ID switch is saved immediately, even if you leave without Save. After 5 wrong PINs sign-in stops for 1 minute. After 8 the PIN is not enough: you need the 8-digit recovery code, then you choose a new PIN. The count stays on the phone.
  • Assign routines, PDF/JSON export, exercise database. Muscle and type stay on the fixed catalog lists. In Trainer → New equipment you can add gym equipment: it shows up when you create an exercise. When you create an exercise you choose the category Fitness, Mobility, Isometric or Pilates: that is the catalog section it appears in.
  • Client registry: from the Home menu, tap Client registry or a client name. The card opens with that client's biometric data. Back returns to the menu you came from.
  • Efficiency (Trainer Pro and Studio): this week's adherence, done and missed sessions, and who to call. The Filters panel at the top works like History and Progress: athlete, trainer (on Studio), chart weeks (4, 8 or 12), and the labels On track, To call, No calendar. Charts start as totals for every visible client. Tap a name, or use the Athlete wheel, to chart only that client (tap the name again, or ✕, to return to the totals). The arrow opens their Progress. On iPhone one chart at a time; on iPad the list sits beside the charts.
    Reloading the demo package on Studio keeps the same demo clients and assigns them to three trainers: the Supervisor, Giulia Ferri and Andrea Conti (staff PIN 1234). Luca belongs to Andrea and is shared with Giulia. Over the last 8 weeks history and the calendar match, so the list shows who is on track, who to call, and who has no calendar. The supervisor sees everyone and signs in with their own PIN, the same as Giulia and Andrea (leaving the wheel on Supervisor is not enough). Signing in as Giulia or Andrea shows only their own clients.
  • Clear Workout History deletes sessions permanently. They stay gone after you close and reopen the app. Demo history comes back only if you load the demo package again.
  • Every catalog entry includes GIF, primary/secondary muscles, silhouette, description and execution tips (same content as the athlete library).
  • Studio: unlimited clients, mobility protocols, multi-trainer, full PDF encyclopedia (entire catalog; muscle segments remain on Pro Plan too).
  • Assignments (supervisor only): More → Trainer Pro → Studio tools → Trainer management. Under the orange button there is the client × trainer table and the legend: one person = owner, two people = shared, a dash = no link. Tap the name for the profile. Tap a cell to make owner, share, or remove (the previous owner no longer follows them; removing an owner parks the client on the supervisor). Tap a column to suspend, reactivate, or open the trainer card. A suspended trainer stays in a column but cannot receive new clients. Under Unassigned, the person icon assigns an owner. The tick on a name selects several clients: then Make owner, Share, Remove and park on the supervisor or Remove share applies to everyone selected. The tick on the Client header selects them all.
  • Print / PDF export of the exercise library (segment manuals or full encyclopedia): paid Pro Plan / Studio subscription only — not during the free trial.
  • In Trainer Pro, actions are split: Backup & restore app (whole app) ≠ Export & import (routines / history).
  • Export / Send Routines and Import Routines: exchange routines between colleagues/devices; import can create missing clients. Paid Pro/Studio only — not during trial.
  • Export / Import history (ZIP): sessions and charts only, not routines. Full backup: disaster recovery for the whole app. Export my data has Import my data beside it (same routines JSON). History/biometrics CSV: paid subscription only — not during the free trial.
  • Routine PDF/image exports use silhouettes and YouTube QR; Gym Visual GIFs stay in-app only (never printed or exported).
  • Single-routine export (table/manual PDF, HD image) and progress reports (JSON/PDF): paid subscription only — not during the free trial.
  • AI credits in trial: max 10 (not the full plan 50/150); paid boosters remain available.

On iPad the trainer area is especially convenient for catalog and handoffs.
