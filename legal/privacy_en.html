PRIVACY POLICY

Application: Stride — running, gym & Apple Watch training
Last updated: 17 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Stride collects, uses, and protects User information. Our philosophy is based on an "Offline-First" approach: almost all data remains physically confined to the User's device.

Brand note: the application is published and identified as Stride (formerly MOVE Fitness / M.O.V.E.). All references to the product in this Privacy Policy mean Stride.


1. DATA COLLECTION AND PROCESSING

Stride is designed to operate without requiring account creation or login. We do not proactively collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers for the App's core functionality. The consent provided through the initial disclaimer authorizes the local data processing necessary for the App to function as described in this policy.

Data entered by the User (e.g., workout details, timers, history) is saved locally on the device via internal databases (e.g., Hive and Shared Preferences). The Developer does not have access to this data and does not store it on proprietary servers.


2. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND THIRD-PARTY DATA TRANSFER

The App includes an "Import from Photo" (AI Wizard) feature. To provide this service, Stride uses Google Generative AI APIs (Google LLC).

  • When the User uses this feature, the selected image is converted and transmitted temporarily and securely to Google's servers for processing and text recognition only.
  • No identifying data associated with the image is transmitted.
  • Users are strongly advised not to capture images containing personal data, faces, ID documents, or sensitive information.

3. HEALTH DATA ACCESS (APPLE HEALTH)

The App, in its versions for wearable devices (e.g., Apple Watch), may request permission to read and/or write data to the Health app (Apple Health).

  • This data is used exclusively to provide real-time feedback during workouts.
  • Non-sharing rule: Data acquired through Apple Health is NOT stored on our servers and is NOT used for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

To ensure proper functionality, the App requires access to:

  • Camera/Photo Library: Exclusively for the "Import from Photo" AI feature.
  • Location / GPS: Outdoor running on Athlete Pro and higher plans: distance, pace, a downsampled route track and a street map on the workout summary. Coordinates and the track stay on the device; they are not uploaded to Stride servers.
  • Map background: To draw streets under the route, the App may request a snapshot or tiles from Apple Maps, Esri or OpenStreetMap. Those providers receive the geographic bounding box, not the full GPS track.
  • Local Storage: To save workout databases and export files.

5. ROLE OF PROFESSIONALS (TRAINERS)

Trainers using the App to manage third-party clients act as the Data Controllers for the data entered. The Developer has no access to or responsibility for the management of such data.


6. PROTECTION OF MINORS

Stride is intended exclusively for an audience aged 18 and over (and in any case, of legal age in their jurisdiction of residence). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware of a minor using the App, we encourage parents to immediately uninstall the App.
