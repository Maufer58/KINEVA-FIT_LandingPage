2. Nature of the service

The application is published as Stride (formerly MOVE Fitness / M.O.V.E.).

Stride is a fitness app (running, gym and Apple Watch training) and workout-planning tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or medical advice.

User-entered “focus areas” / physical limitations are self-declared training preferences, not clinical records. If such data qualify as health data (GDPR Art. 9), processing is based on explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)).

---

3. Categories of data

Account/profile data; workout plans and history; optional body metrics; self-declared focus areas; technical preferences; subscription status (App Store / RevenueCat); HealthKit/Watch data if authorised; GPS coordinates, downsampled route track, distance and pace if outdoor running is authorised; map snapshot/tiles for the street background of the route summary; photos for optional paper-sheet import; AI prompts if AI features are used.

GPS coordinates and the route track stay on the device (and Apple Watch, if used). They are not uploaded to Stride servers. To draw streets under the track, the app may request a map snapshot or tiles from Apple Maps, Esri or OpenStreetMap; those providers receive the geographic bounding box needed to render the map, not the full GPS track.

We do not collect payment card data ourselves (Apple handles payments).

4. Purposes & legal bases

Provide the fitness service (Art. 6(1)(b)); manage subscriptions; Apple Watch sync (consent/authorisation); outdoor GPS distance, pace and route map on Athlete Pro and higher plans (consent/system permission); optional AI features (consent/contract); security and diagnostics (Art. 6(1)(f)); legal compliance (Art. 6(1)(c)).

No sale of personal data to brokers; no third-party advertising profiling by the Controller.

---

5. Storage

Offline-first on device. Retention until deletion/uninstall or erasure request. Store billing records remain with Apple per Apple policies.

6. Recipients

Apple (App Store, HealthKit, Apple Maps snapshots); Esri / OpenStreetMap (street map tiles for the route bounding box); RevenueCat (entitlements); Google Generative AI APIs (optional AI); user-controlled iCloud/device backups.

Extra-EEA transfers use GDPR Chapter V safeguards where applicable.
