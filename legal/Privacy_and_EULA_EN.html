2. Scope and nature of the service

The application is published as KINEVA FIT (formerly MOVE Fitness / M.O.V.E.) on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

KINEVA FIT is a fitness application (running, gym and Apple Watch training) and a workout-organisation tool, including multi-client features for trainers/gyms.
It is not a medical device (MDR / Italian Legislative Decree 46/1997 as amended), and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or medical advice.

“Focus areas” / physical limitations and self-declared conditions entered by the user or trainer are training preferences / declarations, not clinical records. If such data may qualify as health data (GDPR Art. 9), processing takes place only with the data subject’s explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), collected through in-app acceptance flows and system permissions.

4. Purposes of processing

  1. Provide fitness features (routines, timers, history, export, Watch).
  2. Manage athlete/client profiles and training preferences (including multi-trainer).
  3. Sync workouts with Apple Watch (when enabled).
  4. Write completed workouts to Apple Health / Fitness (iOS, if authorised).
  5. Measure distance, pace and route for outdoor runs (Athlete Pro plan or higher).
  6. Show the street map of the route after a session.
  7. Manage subscriptions, device seats, AI credits and add-ons (e.g. Pilates pack).
  8. Protect access to the trainer area / App Lock (PIN, device biometrics).
  9. Improve product stability and security (local technical diagnostics).
  10. Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use data for third-party advertising profiling or sale to data brokers. There is no KINEVA FIT backend that centralises users’ routines, GPS or biometrics.

5. Means and place of storage

  • Offline-first architecture: most data stays on the device (local encrypted Hive database and, where applicable, Keychain / Keystore).
  • GPS coordinates and the route track stay on the device (and on Apple Watch, if used). They are not sent to KINEVA FIT servers.
  • To draw streets under the route, the app may request a map snapshot or tiles from Apple Maps, Esri or OpenStreetMap: the provider receives the geographic area (bounding box) needed for rendering, not the full GPS track.
  • Backup, export or sync occur only when started by the user (File / Email / WhatsApp / ZIP archive to Files/iCloud/Drive) or required by a feature (e.g. WatchConnectivity).
  • The licence seat registry may sync via iCloud Keychain if the user has iCloud Keychain enabled (licence metadata only, not workout routines).
  • Retention period: until deletion of data in the app, uninstall, or an erasure request to the Controller; billing data remains with Apple / Google under their policies.

9. Security and confidentiality

Technical and organisational measures adopted, proportionate to the risk:

  • encryption of the local Hive database (key in Keychain / Keystore);
  • trainer PIN and recovery code stored as hashes (not plaintext);
  • App Lock with timeout and optional unlock via device biometrics (Face ID / fingerprint);
  • selective routine sharing that does not include email, phone, conditions or full client biometrics (id and name only), unless an explicit full trainer export/transfer is used;
  • no KINEVA FIT application server for routines/GPS;
  • cleartext HTTP traffic disabled on the Android client.

No system is 100% secure: protect the device with a passcode/biometrics and do not share unencrypted backups. If the device is compromised or you use export channels you choose (email/WhatsApp), responsibility for that transmission rests with whoever starts the share.
